The Top 3 Bakeries In Charleston

1
Upper King Street

Welton's Tiny Bakeshop

Charleston's most talked-about bakery lives up to the name — a 956-square-foot shop turning out naturally leavened sourdough, flaky croissants, and seasonal kolaches. Expect a line, especially on weekends, and go early for the best selection.

2
471 King Street

Sweet Palm Coffee & Food

This King Street café earns its spot for two things above all: warm, generously glazed cinnamon rolls and tender blueberry muffins that sell out early. Grab one with a coffee and find a seat on King Street to watch the morning go by.

3
Cannonborough-Elliottborough

Sugar Bakeshop

A neighborhood staple on Cannon Street since 2007, known for scratch-made cupcakes in a rotating lineup of flavors, plus saucer-sized oatmeal raisin cookies and the beloved "Charleston Chewies." Come before they sell out in the afternoon.

A Few More Bakeries To Know

West Ashley · 1750 Savannah Hwy

WildFlour Pastry

A West Ashley favorite from pastry chef Lauren Mitterer, best known for Sticky Bun Sundays, cinnamon rolls locals line up for, and a rotating case of scones, quiches, and muffins.

Downtown · 90 Society Street

Christophe Artisan Chocolatier-Pâtissier

A tiny French pâtisserie tucked off King Street, run by a third-generation Toulouse chocolatier. Come for the almond croissants and hand-painted chocolates, stay for the espresso.

French Quarter · 160 E Bay Street

Bakehouse Charleston

A coffee-and-bakery combo on East Bay Street with a wide case of cakes, tarts, muffins, and brownie bars, plus custom cakes for special occasions.

Before You Go

Insider Tips

Go Early

Small-batch bakeries like Welton's and WildFlour bake once and sell out — plan to arrive within the first hour or two of opening for the full case.

Cash & Parking

King Street spots fill up fast on weekend mornings. Metered street parking is common — bring a card or the parking app for the meter, and check hours before you go since several close by early afternoon.

Call Ahead For Orders

Sugar Bakeshop and Sweet Palm both take phone orders for larger quantities — helpful if you're picking up cupcakes or a box of cinnamon rolls for a group.
